THE JOURNEY

From Atlanta to the World Stage

Born in Atlanta and raised in the vibrant streets of Lagos, Nigeria, Davido has orchestrated a musical revolution that transcends continents and cultures. Over a legendary 15-year career, he has single-handedly redefined the global perception of African music.

As the founder and visionary behind Davido Music Worldwide (DMW) record label—established in 2016—he has nurtured and elevated emerging talents while cementing his own legacy as an unstoppable force in the industry.

His guiding philosophy, "We Rise by Lifting Others," reflects a commitment to collective growth and empowerment. Every collaboration, every feature, and every release serves as a testament to his belief that greatness is amplified when shared.

Davido isn't just a musician—he's a cultural ambassador, a tastemaker, and a visionary who continues to shape the sound and spirit of a generation.
